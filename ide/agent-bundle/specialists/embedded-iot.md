# Embedded Systems & IoT Engineering — specialists

Bare-metal firmware, RTOS, microcontroller peripherals, and IoT connectivity/security end to end — from bootloader to cloud telemetry, on constrained hardware.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
