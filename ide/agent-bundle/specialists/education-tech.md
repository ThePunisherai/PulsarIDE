# Education Technology Engineering — specialists

Real software engineering for the education industry -- learning management systems, adaptive learning engines, assessment/proctoring, student information systems, and content-interoperability standards (SCORM/xAPI/LTI). Distinct from Team 19's cross-vertical regulatory-compliance-engineering coverage and Team 44's HR/people-analytics focus; this team owns building actual EdTech products.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
