# Oil & Gas Upstream & Drilling Technology Engineering — specialists

Distinct from Team 82 (Chemical Process & Petrochemical Engineering)'s refinery/midstream-pipeline focus -- this team covers upstream exploration, drilling, and production specifically.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
