# Chemical Process & Petrochemical Engineering Technology — specialists

Chemical and petrochemical plant process-control technology: DCS/batch-process automation, process safety management, refinery optimization, and statutory environmental/safety compliance. Distinct from Team 38 (Manufacturing & Industrial IoT/MES)'s discrete-manufacturing focus and Team 75 (Water & Wastewater Utility)'s municipal-treatment focus -- this team covers continuous/batch chemical-process plants specifically.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
