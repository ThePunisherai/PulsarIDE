# Telecommunications & Networking Engineering — specialists

Carrier-grade and enterprise networking end to end — 5G core/RAN, SDN/NFV, VoIP/IMS, optical transport, and the operational systems (OSS/BSS, provisioning, billing) that run them.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
