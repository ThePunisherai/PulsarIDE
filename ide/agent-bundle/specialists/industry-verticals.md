# Industry & Regulated-Sector Engineering — specialists

Domain-specific engineering for regulated and vertical industries — FinTech, HealthTech, LegalTech, GovTech, EdTech, InsurTech, and PropTech — where the compliance regime is as much a design constraint as the code.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
