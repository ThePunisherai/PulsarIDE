# Automotive Aftermarket & Repair Shop Technology Engineering — specialists

Distinct from Team 29 (Automotive & Mobility Software Engineering)'s OEM/AV-stack/fleet-telematics focus -- this team covers the aftermarket parts and repair-shop side of the industry.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
