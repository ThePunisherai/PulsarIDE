# Geospatial & GIS Engineering — specialists

Spatial data end to end — geocoding, spatial databases, web mapping, and geospatial analysis, from raw survey/satellite data to production location-intelligence systems.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
