# Payments & Fintech Infrastructure Engineering — specialists

Payment processing infrastructure: card networks, gateways, orchestration, fraud, compliance, and settlement -- distinct from Team 20's trading/quant focus and Team 55's insurance focus. Covers building the rails money actually moves on.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
