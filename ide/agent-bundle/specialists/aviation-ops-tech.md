# Aviation Operations Technology Engineering — specialists

Airline and airport operations technology: reservations/PSS, crew scheduling, flight dispatch, MRO, and airport operational systems -- distinct from Team 43 (Aerospace & Space Systems)'s spacecraft/aircraft-manufacturing focus and deliberately non-duplicative of Team 64 (Travel & Hospitality)'s existing GDS/booking-facing airline agents -- this team covers the deeper backend operations layer (MRO, dispatch, ground/station ops, revenue management, inventory control) rather than re-covering booking-integration ground Team 64 already owns.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
