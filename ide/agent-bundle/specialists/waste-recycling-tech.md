# Waste & Recycling Management Technology Engineering — specialists

Waste-management and recycling-facility technology: Materials Recovery Facility (MRF) sorting, waste-to-energy plant control, landfill monitoring, and hauler fleet operations. Distinct from Team 72 (Smart City & Urban Infrastructure)'s civic collection-route-planning focus -- this team covers the facility-level and commercial waste/recycling operations layer.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
