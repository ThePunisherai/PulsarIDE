# Agricultural Technology Engineering — specialists

Precision agriculture and farm-technology software engineering -- crop yield prediction, agricultural IoT/drone/robotics integration, farm management systems, and agri-supply-chain traceability. A real, distinct engineering discipline grounded in precision-agriculture standards and equipment.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
