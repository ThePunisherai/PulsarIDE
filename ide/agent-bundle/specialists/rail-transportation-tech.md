# Rail Transportation Technology Engineering — specialists

Freight and passenger intercity/heavy-rail technology: dispatch and scheduling, Positive Train Control, rolling-stock maintenance, and freight-car tracking. Distinct from Team 72 (Smart City & Urban Infrastructure)'s urban/light-rail signaling focus -- this team covers intercity and freight rail-network operations specifically.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
