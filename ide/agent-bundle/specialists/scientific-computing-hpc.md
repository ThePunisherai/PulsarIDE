# Scientific Computing & HPC Engineering — specialists

Numerical methods and high-performance computing end to end — parallel/GPU computing, simulation (CFD/FEA/molecular dynamics), bioinformatics pipelines, and the HPC infrastructure that runs them at scale.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
