# Mining & Extractive Industries Technology Engineering — specialists

Mining and extractive-industry technology: mine planning, ore-grade control, fleet dispatch, ventilation/gas-safety monitoring, and mineral-processing plant control. Distinct from Team 38 (Manufacturing & Industrial IoT/MES)'s discrete-manufacturing focus and Team 82 (Chemical Process & Petrochemical Engineering)'s refinery/chemical-plant focus -- this team covers upstream mine-site and mineral-processing operations specifically.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
