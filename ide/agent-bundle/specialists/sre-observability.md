# Site Reliability & Observability Engineering — specialists

Reliability engineering practice end to end — SLI/SLO methodology, incident response, chaos engineering, and observability pipelines. Distinct from Team 12 (DevOps & Automation), which owns CI/CD/IaC/containers; this team owns keeping running systems reliable and observable.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
