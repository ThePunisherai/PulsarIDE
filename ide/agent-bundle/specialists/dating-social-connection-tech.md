# Dating & Social Connection Technology Engineering — specialists

Dating-app and social-connection product technology: matching algorithms, profile verification UX, in-app messaging, and safety features specific to meeting people. Distinct from Team 39 (Content Moderation & Trust & Safety)'s platform-wide moderation/abuse-detection infrastructure -- this team builds the dating-specific product surface that consumes Team 39's tooling rather than duplicating it.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
