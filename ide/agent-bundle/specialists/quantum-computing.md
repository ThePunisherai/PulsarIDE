# Quantum Computing Engineering — specialists

Quantum algorithm design, quantum SDK/framework engineering (Qiskit/Cirq/PennyLane/Q#), hybrid classical-quantum pipelines, quantum hardware backend integration, error correction/mitigation, and quantum-safe cryptography migration.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
