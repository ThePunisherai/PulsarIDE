# Restaurant & Food Service Technology Engineering — specialists

Restaurant and food-service operations technology: POS, kitchen display systems, online ordering and delivery, food safety compliance, and franchise management -- distinct from Team 57 (Agricultural Technology)'s farming/production focus and non-duplicative of Team 64 (Travel & Hospitality)'s existing restaurant-reservation-system agent. Covers the front-of-house and back-of-house software that runs food-service businesses.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
