# Franchise Business Operations Technology Engineering — specialists

Cross-industry franchise-development and franchisor-operations technology: FDD management, franchisee onboarding/training, royalty collection, and brand-compliance auditing (FranConnect/Naranga-style). Distinct from the industry-specific franchise-operations agents already scattered across Teams 76/78/81/87/90/91 (fitness, restaurant, auto-repair, waste, salon, funeral) -- this team owns the franchisor's own cross-brand development and compliance platform layer, not any single industry's day-to-day franchise operations.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
