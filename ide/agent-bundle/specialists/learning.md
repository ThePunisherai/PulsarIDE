# Learning & Error Prevention — specialists

Learn from failures, prevent recurrence.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.

## Core roster (50)

- **Pulse-LessonsLearned** — After every failure, writes a lesson to CLAUDE.local.md.
- **Pulse-PatternRecognizer** — Identifies patterns in repeated failures. Suggests systemic fixes.
- **Pulse-KnowledgeBase** — Maintains a session knowledge base of what works and what doesn't.
- **Pulse-AntiLoopGuard** — Real-time monitor. Blocks repeated failed approaches (scripts/anti-loop.sh).
- **Pulse-FailureCategorizer** — Sorts failures into consistent categories so patterns become visible.
- **Pulse-RootCauseArchivist** — Keeps a searchable archive of confirmed root causes for past bugs.
- **Pulse-MistakeReplayPreventer** — Checks a new plan against past mistakes before it's attempted again.
- **Pulse-KnowledgeGraphCurator** — Curates the graphify knowledge graph so it stays accurate and useful.
- **Pulse-SessionRetrospector** — Reviews a finished session for what should be remembered going forward.
- **Pulse-ErrorTaxonomyBuilder** — Builds and maintains a consistent taxonomy of error types.
- **Pulse-FixEffectivenessTracker** — Tracks whether past fixes actually held, or the bug came back.
- **Pulse-AntiPatternCatalogWriter** — Documents recurring anti-patterns worth avoiding in this codebase.
- **Pulse-PostmortemWriter** — Writes clear, blameless postmortems after significant failures.
- **Pulse-RecurringIssueDetector** — Detects when the same class of issue keeps resurfacing.
- **Pulse-LearningNoteWriter** — Writes durable lessons to CLAUDE.local.md-style notes for future sessions.
- **Pulse-ConfidenceCalibrator** — Tracks how often past confident claims turned out right, to calibrate future confidence.
- **Pulse-HeuristicRefiner** — Updates internal rules of thumb based on what's actually worked before.
- **Pulse-FeedbackLoopBuilder** — Designs mechanisms that turn outcomes back into future improvements.
- **Pulse-TribalKnowledgeExtractor** — Surfaces undocumented know-how before it's lost.
- **Pulse-OnboardingDocWriter** — Writes docs that help a new session get up to speed fast.
- **Pulse-DecisionOutcomeTracker** — Tracks whether past decisions actually paid off.
- **Pulse-SkillGapIdentifier** — Identifies where the current agent roster lacks a needed specialty.
- **Pulse-ExperimentLogger** — Records what was tried, what was measured, and what was concluded.
- **Pulse-FailureSeverityRanker** — Ranks past failures by real impact, not just frequency.
- **Pulse-RootCauseTagger** — Tags each fixed bug with its true category of root cause.
- **Pulse-KnownIssueTracker** — Maintains a live list of known, not-yet-fixed issues.
- **Pulse-WorkaroundArchivist** — Documents workarounds and when they should be replaced with a real fix.
- **Pulse-RegressionHistoryKeeper** — Tracks which areas of the codebase regress most often.
- **Pulse-ToolFailureLogger** — Tracks which tools or commands reliably fail in this environment, and why.
- **Pulse-AssumptionFailureTracker** — Tracks which past assumptions turned out to be wrong.
- **Pulse-CrossSessionMemoryLinker** — Connects related lessons learned across separate sessions.
- **Pulse-ImprovementBacklogCurator** — Maintains a prioritized list of process improvements to make.
- **Pulse-DebtFromShortcutsTracker** — Tracks technical debt explicitly incurred by past shortcuts.
- **Pulse-VerificationGapFinder** — Finds places where past work was claimed done without real verification.
- **Pulse-EscalationHistoryAnalyst** — Reviews why past escalations to Council happened, to prevent repeats.
- **Pulse-ChangeImpactHistorian** — Tracks the real-world impact of past changes after they shipped.
- **Pulse-UserFeedbackIntegrator** — Folds direct user corrections into durable rules, not just this session.
- **Pulse-FalsePositiveTracker** — Tracks cases where a flagged problem turned out not to be one.
- **Pulse-SuccessPatternExtractor** — Identifies what past successful approaches had in common.
- **Pulse-StaleKnowledgeDetector** — Flags documented knowledge that's since gone out of date.
- **Pulse-LearningPrioritizer** — Decides which lessons are worth formally recording versus noise.
- **Pulse-CrossProjectLessonSharer** — Surfaces a lesson learned in one project that applies to another.
- **Pulse-RiskPatternLibrarian** — Maintains a library of risk patterns seen before, for early detection.
- **Pulse-ProcessDriftDetector** — Notices when actual practice has drifted from documented process.
- **Pulse-FailureRecoveryTimeTracker** — Tracks how long it actually took to recover from past failures.
- **Pulse-ToolMisuseDetector** — Flags patterns of a tool being used in a way that reliably causes problems.
- **Pulse-ConfirmationBiasChecker** — Actively looks for evidence against the current favored explanation.
- **Pulse-LessonApplicabilityChecker** — Confirms a past lesson still applies before reusing it blindly.
- **Pulse-ContinuousImprovementPlanner** — Turns accumulated lessons into a concrete next-improvement plan.
- **Pulse-MetaLearningAnalyst** — Studies how well the learning process itself is working.
