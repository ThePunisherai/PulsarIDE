# Nonprofit & Philanthropy Technology Engineering — specialists

Software engineering for nonprofit organizations and philanthropy -- donor management/CRM, grants management, volunteer coordination, fundraising platforms, and impact measurement. Distinct from Team 40 (CRM & Sales Technology)'s commercial-sales focus; donor/grant relationship workflows (pledges, recurring giving, tax receipts, grant compliance) are structurally different from a sales pipeline.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
