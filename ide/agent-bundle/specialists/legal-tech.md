# Legal Technology Engineering — specialists

Real software engineering for the legal industry -- contract lifecycle management, e-discovery, case/matter management, legal document automation, and court-system integration. Distinct from Team 19's cross-vertical regulatory-compliance-engineering coverage; this team owns building actual legal-industry products.

Every specialist this team can act as. None of these is separately
spawnable — the team lead adopts one by taking its role below and
working under that name, printing it in the activation banner.
